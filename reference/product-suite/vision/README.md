# Evolith Product Suite — Vision

> **Bilingual navigation:** [Versión en Español](./README.es.md)

**Classification:** Product Suite Vision  
**Owner:** Evolith Product Governance

This area contains the portfolio-level vision of the Evolith Product Suite. It explains what products exist, why they exist, how they relate, and which outcomes the Suite intends to deliver.

> **Goal:** state why the Suite exists and which outcomes it must deliver.
>
> **Objectives:** define the product portfolio, its relationships, and its adoption stages — without entering Core rules or product internals.

## Boundary

This area may define portfolio structure, Suite-level value, product relationships, adoption stages, and commercial direction. Universal architecture principles belong in Evolith Core. Internal product implementation belongs in `reference/products/`.
