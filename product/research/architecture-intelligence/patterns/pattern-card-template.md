# Pattern Card Template

> Standard structure for documenting curated architectural patterns in Evolith.

## Pattern Name

Short, precise name.

## Source

Where the idea comes from.

Examples:
- book
- talk
- video
- repository
- internal production lesson
- UMS evidence

## Problem

Describe the architectural problem being solved.

## Context

When this problem usually appears.

## Solution

Describe the recommended approach.

## Benefits

List the main benefits.

## Tradeoffs

List costs, risks, complexity, and operational impact.

## Evolith Position

Allowed values:
- Recommended
- Conditional
- Experimental
- Not Recommended

## Adoption Level

Allowed values:
- Team
- Product
- Enterprise
- Research

## AI Impact

Explain how this pattern affects AI-assisted engineering.

## Related ADRs

Link controlling ADRs or ADR candidates.

## Related Standards

Link governance or engineering standards.

## Validation Rules

Define what must be verified before applying the pattern.

## Anti-Patterns

List common incorrect implementations.
