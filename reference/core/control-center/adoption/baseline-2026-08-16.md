## Explicitly rejected as metrics

**`traffic/clones` — 14,031 clones / 4,336 uniques in 14 days.** These are CI runners, not
humans. It is the largest number available and it measures nothing about adoption; quoting
it would be the most flattering and least honest figure in this document.

**Stars as the primary metric.** Cheap to acquire, uncorrelated with use, and it does not
change the bus factor.

**Cumulative download totals.** Monotonic, so they only ever go up, which makes them useless
as a signal.
