## Purpose

A Functional Story describes a user-facing or business-facing capability in business language. It defines actors, flows, business rules, acceptance criteria, and traceability without prescribing implementation.

## Authoring Rules

- Keep the story readable by Product Owners, Business Analysts, QA, and Technology Leaders.
- Express behavior and rules before technical implementation.
- Link every Functional Story to a parent PRD and downstream Technical Stories.
- Follow the Functional Story Writing Standard before submitting the story for Design Baseline review.
